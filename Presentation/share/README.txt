WBA ontology viewer (result of the finished lite run, 2026-10-15)

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click ontology_viewer.html. It opens in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
  It is ONE file: no install, no server, no internet needed.

WHAT YOU SEE
  Overview - totals and reference classes | Class tree | Graph (click a class; "Export PNG" saves a readable picture)
  Properties | Facts (every fact names its source document, section and page).
  Blue = terms from the reference ontology, orange = terms added from the NBN WBA documents.
  Tip: the sun/moon button switches light/dark; search box supports  kind:class  and  doc:"Price List".

wba_ontology_merged.ttl
  The same ontology as Turtle (open in Protege, or any RDF tool). Four documents merged:
  Product Technical Specification, Product Description, Price List, Service Levels Schedule.

CAVEATS (please read)
  - Final result of the run; 46 of the 51 chosen pages made it in (Tech Spec pages 32, 41, 51, Price List page 8 failed; Product Description page 9 added nothing).
  - Only the pages that matter most for NNI / CVC / AVC / bearer / service-level concepts were used, not whole documents.
  - Price List facts mostly lack a source clause (known bug); some classes are near-duplicates (e.g. Cl_AVC and AVC).
  - Generated by an LLM pipeline (DeepSeek V4 Flash) and checked automatically, not reviewed by a human.
